## 📋 How to Use This Manual

1. **Read slides 1–8** in full before your first annotation session
2. Keep **slides 6 and 9** open in a separate tab during annotation as a quick reference
3. Complete the **20-case calibration batch** before starting the full dataset
4. Attend the calibration debrief session with the lead researcher
5. Your Label Studio link and login credentials will be sent to you separately via private message

## ⚠️ Confidentiality Notice

- All annotation materials, case screenshots, and labels are strictly confidential
- Do **not** share this manual or any case images outside the annotation team
- Do **not** upload any case screenshots to AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.)
- Your labels, rationales, and confidence scores may be quoted in the thesis **without identifying which annotator provided them**
