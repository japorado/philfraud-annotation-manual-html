A1 · Binary Label

Fraud vs. Safe — The Key Distinction

🚨
FRAUDULENT

Designed to deceive — aims to extract money, credentials, OTPs, or personal data from the target.

✅
SAFE

Legitimate content from a real source — even if it uses urgency language or promotional offers.

⚠️
The Golden Rule

A real GCash promo with a countdown timer → Safe. A fake GCash promo that steals your MPIN → Fraud. The difference is intent and authenticity, not tone alone.

💬

When in doubt, ask: Would a legitimate Philippine institution (BSP-regulated bank, DTI-registered company, SEC-registered entity) plausibly send or post this content through this channel?

A2 · Fraud Category

Types of Fraud to Identify

Only assign a fraud category if Binary Label = Fraudulent. If Safe → select Legitimate.

📨
Phishing / Smishing

Stealing credentials, OTPs, or personal info via SMS, email, Messenger, Telegram.

↳ OTP / password request
↳ Fake login page links
↳ "Verify now or lose access"
🎭
Impersonation

False identity — fake brand, fake person, fake official, cloned Facebook pages.

↳ Fake celebrity endorsements
↳ Cloned government pages
↳ Deepfake / AI-generated content
💰
Loan / Investment

Fraudulent financial products — fake loans, Ponzi, pyramid, fake SEC certificates.

↳ "Double your money in 7 days"
↳ Fake DTI / SEC approval
↳ Upfront fees before loan release
✅

Legitimate — Select this when Binary Label is Safe. It means the content is from a real, trustworthy source.

A3 · Difficulty Tier

How Hard Is This Case to Spot?

Rate based on this single screenshot only — not the full post context.

Easy ~1 min

3+ independent, self-evident signals. A non-expert spots it in 15–30 seconds.

✓ Low-res / wrong logo
✓ Explicit OTP / PIN request
✓ Gmail sender on bank notification
✓ Prize claim + urgency + link
Medium ~2 min

1–2 signals needing inference or Taglish knowledge to decode.

✓ Urgency without brand impersonation
✓ Subtle Taglish scam phrases
✓ Promotional tone — could go either way
Hard 3–5 min

Minimal or sophisticated signals that could fool even careful observers.

✓ Pixel-accurate brand clone
✓ Edited transaction record
✓ Looks entirely legitimate in isolation
📸

Multi-image post rule: If the image looks fine in isolation but is suspicious only because of other images in the post → assign Medium or Hard (never Easy). Note this in your Rationale.

Section B · Signal Inventory

Common Fraud Signals — Quick Reference

👁️ Visual
Stretched / low-res brand logo
Red/orange urgency design + countdown
Layout cloning (GCash, BDO, Maya)
QR code in unexpected context
Mismatched fonts / edited timestamp
💬 Textual
"Mag-claim na" / urgency phrases
"Nanalo ka!" / peso prize claims
Authority name drops (GCash, SSS, BSP)
OTP, PIN, password request
Gmail/Yahoo sender for "bank" notif
🏗️ Structural
Bank branding in SMS / Messenger
No footer / no privacy policy
bit.ly / tinyurl / non-brand domain
Fake SEC / DTI certificate
Fake crypto / investment dashboard
📲 GCash: Official notifs only via app or sender "GCash". Never asks MPIN via SMS.
🏦 Banks: BDO/BPI send via registered sender IDs or @bdo.com.ph — never via Messenger.
🏛️ Gov't: BSP / SSS / PhilHealth never send prizes or loan approvals via SMS or Facebook.
Section D + A4

Rationale & Confidence

📝 How to Write a Good Rationale
✅ GOOD EXAMPLE

"The SMS uses GCash sender ID but contains a bit.ly link and urgency phrase 'Mag-redeem na ngayon' — three independent signals converging on smishing."

❌ What to Avoid
🚫 "Looks like a scam" — too vague
🚫 "This is fraud because it's fraudulent" — circular
🚫 "This is phishing" — just the category name
💡 Confidence Levels
High

Evidence is clear. You are not second-guessing your label.

Medium

Reasonably sure, but 1–2 signals are ambiguous.

Low

Genuinely uncertain — borderline case. Low confidence is valuable data!

Annotation Procedure

Step-by-Step: Every Case, Every Time

1
View Full Image

Zoom in. Read all text carefully. Check sender, URLs, logos, and the nature of the request.

2
Assign Binary Label

Is this trying to deceive me into giving up credentials, money, or personal info? → Fraud or Safe

3
Assign Fraud Category

Credential theft → Phishing/Smishing · False identity → Impersonation · Financial exploit → Loan/Investment · If Safe → Legitimate

4
Count Fraud Signals

3+ clear = Easy · 1–2 with inference = Medium · Sophisticated / minimal = Hard

5
Write Rationale

Name the 1–2 most important specific signals. 2–4 sentences. Reference Section B. Be precise.

6
Rate Confidence

Be honest. High = certain · Medium = mostly sure · Low = genuinely borderline. Low confidence cases are not failures — they are valuable.

✅

Step 7 — Submit. Click Submit in Label Studio. Do not change labels after submission unless you accidentally clicked the wrong button.

Edge Cases

Tricky Scenarios & How to Handle Them

🛒 Aggressive-but-Legitimate Marketing

Real Shopee/GCash promo with countdown timer, but authentic branding and no credential input → Safe. Urgency alone ≠ Fraud.

📸 Screenshot of a Screenshot

Label the inner screenshot content (the scam message), not the outer framing. Someone reporting a scam does not make it Safe.

✂️ Partial / Cropped Screenshot

Annotate visible content only. If too much context is missing → best judgment + Hard + Low confidence + note in Rationale.

📰 Government Warning / News Article

A legit BSP advisory about scams → Safe. A scam shown inside a news article for educational purposes → Safe. Label the purpose, not the inner content.

🤖 Deepfake / Celebrity Endorsement

Even if you can't confirm it's AI-generated, an implausible celebrity/official endorsement of a product → Fraud + Impersonation + Hard.

⬛ Blank / Corrupted Image

Do not assign a label. Write SKIP - [reason] in the Rationale field + Low confidence. Lead researcher will review.

Before You Begin

📖

Read the full manual before your first annotation. Sections 1–6 cover all definitions and procedures.

🎯

Complete the 20-case calibration batch first. Wait for the lead researcher's debrief before starting the full batch.

🔒

All materials are confidential. Do not share screenshots or upload them to any AI tool (ChatGPT, etc.).

🚫

Don't discuss individual case labels with other annotators until post-annotation analysis is complete.

🧠

Take breaks. Do not annotate more than 100 cases per session. Fatigue degrades label quality.

💬

Message the lead researcher whenever you're unsure about a case. No question is too small.
